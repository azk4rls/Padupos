# PRODUCT_SPEC.md

PADUPOS — Product Specification

## CORE PRODUCT

PADUPOS is a serious global-first AI Business OS (not merely a cashier). Core modules:
- POS (Point of Sale) with cash register shifts, cash movements, reconciliation
- Products (catalog, pricing, variants)
- Inventory (stock tracking, movements, low stock alerts)
- Purchases (procurement, suppliers)
- Suppliers (vendor management)
- Finance (accounts, journals, trial balance, expenses)
- Reports (sales, profit/loss, inventory)
- Dashboard (business metrics)
- AI Insights (grounded business insights)
- ML Forecast (data-sufficient forecasting)
- Offline POS (cash sales with sync)
- Subscription/Plans (entitlements)


## PHILOSOPHY

- Serious, practical, global-first
- Real data only; no gimmicks
- Low infrastructure cost
- Useful repeatedly
- Built for small and growing businesses (primary target)
- Indonesia as initial market, extensible globally via configuration


## PLANS (Product Spec Only)

Planned tiers (product specification, not security enforcement in frontend): Free, Pro, Business, Enterprise. Pricing not finalized; do not treat as authoritative for business logic enforcement beyond product spec.
